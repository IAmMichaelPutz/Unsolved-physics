Particle Collisions and Mean Free Path

Cross-sectional Analysis

Where:

  • σ := cross-sectional area for the collision ("scattering cross section")
  • λmfp := mean free path
$$V_{mfp} = \pi \cdot \sigma \cdot \lambda_{mfp}$$
Mean Free Path Volume
$$V_0 \simeq N \cdot V_{0,mfp} \Rightarrow V_{0,mfp} = \frac{V_0}{N} =: \frac{1}{n}$$
Volume Relations
$$\lambda_{mfp} = \frac{1}{\pi n \sigma}$$
Mean Free Path

Example: Air at Room Temperature

  • σ ≈ (5Å)²
  • pV₀ = NkBT ⟹ $\frac{N}{V_0} = n = \frac{p}{k_BT}$ ⟹ λmfp ≈ 50nm
  • ⟨v⟩ = $\sqrt{\frac{8k_BT}{\pi m}}$ ≈ 600 m/s
$$\text{collision time: } t_c = \frac{\lambda_{mfp}}{\langle v \rangle} = 10^{-10}s$$

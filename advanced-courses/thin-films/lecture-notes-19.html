3.1 Evaporation

  • Easiest form of PVD
  • Controlled transfer of atoms in vapor form from a heated source to the substrate
  • Source is heated until it evaporates (or sublimates)
  • Preferred method for optical coatings
  • Atomistic film growth

Can/should we boil the material?

Usually a melt is just fine.

  • Melting point of Au: ~1064°C
  • Boiling point: ~2836°C → not really feasible

3.1.1 Evaporation Rates

Observations:

  1. Evaporation is not stopped by insufficient heat supply
  2. The rate is proportional to the difference of the equilibrium pressure of the evaporant $P_e$ and the hydrostatic pressure acting on the evaporant $P_h$

Any given chemical element has a specific ability to evaporate at a given temperature. The maximum rate is attained when the number of evaporant atoms corresponds to the equilibrium vapor pressure (and no atom returns to the source).

$\phi_e = \alpha_e \frac{P_e-P_h}{\sqrt{2\pi mRT}} = \alpha_e N_A \frac{P_e-P_h}{\sqrt{2\pi MRT}}$

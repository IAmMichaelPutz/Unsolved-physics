2. Vacuum Science and Technology

In most cases the thin film deposition process requires low ambient pressure and vacuum conditions:

  • reduces contamination of thin film material
  • allows for controlled mass transfer

2.1 Kinetic Theory of Gases

Gas at given temperature → random motion of particles (Maxwell-Boltzmann)

$$E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$$
Equation 2.1: Kinetic Energy
$$\text{This energy is reached with probability } \exp\left(-\frac{E_{kin}}{k_BT}\right)$$
Equation 2.2: Energy Probability

Question: how many particles will have velocities in the range [v, v+dv]?

Velocity Distributions:

$$dN \propto N \cdot \exp\left(-\frac{mv^2}{2k_BT}\right) \cdot dv$$
Equation 2.3: Velocity Distribution
$$f_{3D}(v) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{2\pi}}\left(\frac{m}{k_BT}\right)^{3/2}v^2\exp\left(-\frac{mv^2}{2k_BT}\right)$$
Equation 2.4: 3D Velocity Distribution Function

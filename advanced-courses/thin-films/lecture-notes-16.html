Flow Regimes

Different flow regimes:

  • Viscous flow (seconds to minutes)
  • Intermediate flow
  • Molecular flow (hours)

Vacuum Pumps

A) Gas Transfer Pumps

Removes gas molecules from the chamber permanently

  • Lubricated pumps: Need liquids for preventing mechanical damage/sealing
  • Oil-free pumps: Used as backup pumps in large systems
  • Turbo molecular pump:
    • Main idea: Change of momentum of residual gas molecules towards exhaust direction
    • Rotation speed: 20,000 - 80,000 rpm (~300 - 1,000 Hz)

B) Gas Entrapment

Particles are condensed or chemically/physically bound to surfaces inside the pump

  • Needs occasional recovery/replacement of entrapment area

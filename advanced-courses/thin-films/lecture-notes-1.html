0. Preface/Introduction

~ 1000 BC: thin gold films < 200nm leaf platings in Egypt
~ 1650 AD: interference patterns of oil in water explained
~ 1940 AD: use of thin films for optical, electronic, mechanical and protective applications
~ 1960 AD: thin film semiconductor technology
~ 1930 AD: thin film high-Tc superconductors
~ 2004 AD: TFT displays ...

Why to use thin films:

  • interface engineering
  • adding properties: cost reduction, catalysts, decoration
  • protective coatings
  • miniaturization
  • optical properties

→ Typical dimensions of a thin film: ≤ 1µm

We will not talk about free-standing films (i.e. foils), thin films often show very different properties compared to bulk or free standing material!

Film Thickness Uniformity (continued)

Experimental Observation

Oriented material transfer is described by a cosine law

\[ \frac{dM_s}{dA_s} = \frac{(n+1)M_e}{2\pi r^2} \cos\theta \cos^n\phi \]
Material transfer equation with orientation factor

For a plane source:

\[ \frac{dM_s}{dA_s} = \frac{M_e}{πr^2}\cos(\theta)\cos(\phi) \]

Source Configurations

  • Plate: n = 1
  • Boat: n = 1...3
  • Cup: n = 3...7
  • Knudsen cell: n >> 15 (e.g. for dangerous or very rare materials like uranium)

Thickness Uniformity Analysis

\[ dM_s = S \cdot dA_s \cdot d \]
Mass-thickness relationship

Example: Point Source

\[ d = \frac{1}{S}\frac{dM_s}{dA_s} = \frac{1}{S}\frac{M_e}{4\pi r^2}\cos\theta \] \[ d = \frac{1}{S}\frac{M_e}{4\pi}\frac{h}{(x^2+h^2)^{3/2}} \]

Where:

  • S: density
  • d: thickness
  • h: height
  • x: lateral distance

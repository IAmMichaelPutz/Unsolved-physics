Average Velocities

$$f_{1D}(v) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{2\pi}}\left(\frac{m}{k_BT}\right)^{1/2}\exp\left(-\frac{mv^2}{2k_BT}\right)$$
1D Velocity Distribution

Average Velocity Calculations:

$$\text{Average: }\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} |v| f(v)\, dv = \langle|v|\rangle = \sqrt{\frac{2k_BT}{\pi m}} \text{ (1D)}$$
Average Velocity (1D)
$$\text{Root mean square: }\left[\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} v^2 f(v)\, dv\right]^{1/2} = \sqrt{\langle v^2\rangle} = \sqrt{\frac{k_BT}{m}} = v_{rms}$$
RMS Velocity

For 3D case: $\sqrt{\frac{3k_BT}{m}}$

Gas Pressure and Momentum Transfer

Ideal gas: $pV = Nk_BT$

Calculate momentum transfer from gas to wall (non-directional):

  • Momentum change at given wall: $\Delta p_m = 2mv_x \sim 2mv_{rms,x}$
  • Time between collisions: $\Delta t = \frac{\Delta L}{v_{rms,x}}$
$$F = F_m = \frac{\Delta p_m}{\Delta t} = \frac{2mv_{rms,x}}{\Delta L/v_{rms,x}} = \frac{mv_{rms,x}^2}{L}$$
Force on Wall

Extended to 3D, N particles:

$$F = \frac{N \cdot mv_{rms}^2}{3 \cdot L}$$
3D Force
$$p = \frac{F}{A} = \frac{N}{3}\frac{mv_{rms}^2}{L^3} = \frac{N}{3}\frac{mv_{rms}^2}{V_0}$$
Pressure Definition

The pressure inside a vacuum chamber is defined as a function of temperature or particle motion

Pressure Units

  • [p] = 1 Pa = 1 Pascal = 1 N/m²
  • 1 bar = 100,000 Pa = 1000 mbar
  • 1 atm = 1013.25 mbar
  • 1 torr = 1mm Hg = 133.322 Pa
  • 1 bar = 750 torr

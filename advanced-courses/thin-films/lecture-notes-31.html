Sputtering Yield Analysis

Definition and Parameters

\[ N_A = n_{gas} \cdot S \cdot C \]
where C is the ratio of target concentration

Reported Values

  • Full range: \(10^{-5} \leq S \leq 10^3\)
  • Typical values: \(0.1 \leq S \leq 10\)
Note: Sputtering is a complex process with low/mid/high energy ranges, each with individual mechanisms. → We will approach it phenomenologically
\[ S \propto \rho_n \cdot \delta_n(E) \cdot \lambda_s \]
where: \(\rho_n\) = atomic density, \(\delta_n(E)\) = energy loss in target, \(\lambda_s\) = (surface) binding energy

Energy Considerations

Low Energy Case:

If incident particle energy is too low:

  • No momentum transfer to target atoms
  • Result: No sputtering occurs

High Energy Case:

If incident particle energy is too high:

  • Results in implantation
  • No effective sputtering occurs

Summary

  • AC/RF sputtering enables the use of insulating targets
  • Magnetic field confinement improves efficiency
  • Sputtering yield depends on multiple factors including energy ranges
  • System design requires careful consideration of energy levels and target materials

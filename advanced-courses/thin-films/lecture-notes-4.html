a closed system

$$ G = U + PV - TS $$
H = enthalpy i.e. stored energy

if a system changes from initial state (i) to a final state (f) at constant pressure and temperature:

$$ \Delta G = G_f - G_i = \Delta H - T\Delta S $$
Spontaneous reactions occur if $\Delta G < 0$
(at const temperature and pressure)

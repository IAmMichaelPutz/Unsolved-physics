1. Review of Material Science

1.1 Structure of crystalline solids

→ 14 distinguishable crystal structures (Bravais lattices)

crystal planes are classified by so-called Miller indices: h,k,l

$(h k l)$: plane

$[h k l]$: direction

$\{h k l\}$: all planes with equal symmetry

not always normal to surface

1.2 Defects in Solids

a) 0-dimensional defects:

point defects, vacancies, impurities, metal deficiencies, etc.

point defects are thermodynamically driven

→ fraction of persistent point defects f

$$ f = \exp\left(-\frac{E_f}{k_B T}\right) $$

$E_f \approx 1\text{eV}$ : @ 300K: $f \sim 10^{-17}$

@ 1000K: $f \sim 10^{-5}$

b) 1-dimensional defects

→ not caused by thermodynamics

it is possible to grow a material without dislocations
but not without vacancies

Example: Surface Coverage by Water Monolayer

How long will it take for a surface to be covered by a monolayer of water?

Assumptions:

  1. All incoming water molecules stay on surface
  2. No stacking of water molecules

Coverage area: (2Å)²

$$\text{Coverage density: } a = 10^{15} \frac{\text{molecules}}{\text{cm}^2}$$ $$\text{Coverage time: } t_c = \frac{a}{\phi}$$ $$\text{Ambient conditions: } \phi = \frac{1013\text{ mbar}}{\sqrt{2\pi \cdot 18u \cdot k_B \cdot 300K}} \simeq 3 \cdot 10^{25} \frac{1}{m^2s}$$

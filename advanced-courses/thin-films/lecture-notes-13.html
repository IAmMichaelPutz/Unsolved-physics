2.2 Gas Flow Regimes

Important because: inappropriate system design limits performance, pumping speed, film quality, etc.

Types of Flow:

Viscous Flow

Laminar Flow

Molecular Flow

Gas flow regime is dependent on chamber dimension D

\[ Kn = \frac{\lambda_{mfp}}{D} \]

Knudsen number

Reminder:

\[ \lambda_{mfp} = \frac{1}{n\pi\sigma} \propto \frac{T}{P} \] \[ n = \frac{N}{V} = \frac{P}{k_BT} \]

Flow Regimes:

  1. Kn > 1: Molecular flow regime

    Mean free path is large compared to chamber dimensions

  2. Kn < 0.01: Viscous flow regime

    Collisions and gas transport via momentum transfer

  3. Intermediate regime

    Mix of molecular and viscous flow

Typical Setup

Typical pressure ranges in HV → D ~0.5m

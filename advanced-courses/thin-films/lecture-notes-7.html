$$II \quad \text{from} \quad (*):$$ $$dU = TdS + SdT - pdV - Vdp + \mu dN + Nd\mu + \gamma dA + Ad\gamma$$
Should be equal

⇒ $dU = A\sigma_{ij}d\epsilon_{ij} = SdT - Vdp + Nd\mu + \gamma dA + Ad\gamma$

This equation holds for all regions and thus especially also for the surface: $V_s, N_s$

$$\Rightarrow A\sigma_{ij}d\epsilon_{ij} = Ad\gamma + S_sdT - V_sdp + N_sd\mu + \gamma dA$$

surface very small, $V_s$ and $N_s \rightarrow 0$

$dA = Ad\epsilon_{ij}$

$$\Rightarrow Ad\gamma + S_sdT + A(\gamma - \sigma_{ij})d\epsilon_{ij} = 0$$

Compare this to total change of surface tension:

$$\gamma = \gamma(T, \epsilon_{ij})$$ $$d\gamma = \frac{\partial \gamma}{\partial T}dT + \frac{\partial \gamma}{\partial \epsilon_{ij}}d\epsilon_{ij} \quad|\cdot A$$
$$Ad\gamma - A\frac{\partial \gamma}{\partial T}dT - A\frac{\partial \gamma}{\partial \epsilon_{ij}}d\epsilon_{ij} = 0$$
$$S_s = -A\frac{\partial \gamma}{\partial T}$$
Surface entropy
$$A(\gamma - \sigma_{ij}) = -A\frac{\partial \gamma}{\partial \epsilon_{ij}}$$
$$\sigma_{ij} = \gamma + \frac{\partial \gamma}{\partial \epsilon_{ij}}$$
$\leftarrow$ surface stress $\quad\quad\quad$ $\leftarrow$ surface tension

Important:

Surface tension and surface stress are not identical!

in liquid: $\frac{\partial \gamma}{\partial \epsilon_{ij}} = 0$ ⇒ no difference between stress and strain

in solids: $\frac{\partial \gamma}{\partial \epsilon_{ij}} \neq 0$ ⇒ buckling of surfaces

$$\text{if} \quad \frac{\partial \gamma}{\partial \epsilon_{ij}} < 0 \quad \Rightarrow \quad \sigma_{ij} < \gamma$$
⇒ introduction of dislocations

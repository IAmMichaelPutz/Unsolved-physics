Problem 10: Clausius-Clapeyron Analysis

\[p(T) \propto \exp\left(-\frac{\Delta H}{RT}\right)\]
Clausius-Clapeyron Equation

Rates are proportional to vapor pressure. Rate ratio R:

\[R \propto \Phi \propto p(T) \propto \exp\left(-\frac{\Delta H}{RT}\right)\]

Given Values:

  • ΔHFe ~ 340 kJ/mol
  • T₁ = 1823 K
  • T₂ = T₁ + 100K = 1923 K
\[\frac{R_2}{R_1} = \frac{\exp\left(-\frac{\Delta H}{RT_2}\right)}{\exp\left(-\frac{\Delta H}{RT_1}\right)} = \exp\left[\frac{\Delta H}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T_1} - \frac{1}{T_2}\right)\right] \approx 3.21\]

Since rates are proportional to area (R ∝ Φ·A ∝ A):

\[\frac{R_2}{R_1} = 3.21 = \frac{A_2}{A_1} \Rightarrow A_2 = 3.21\text{ cm}^2 \text{ (given } A_1 = 1\text{ cm}^2)\]

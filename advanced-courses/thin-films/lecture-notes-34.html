4.6 Some Last Thoughts on Sputtering

(i) Target Integrity in Magnetron Sputtering

  • Only 10-30% of target is actually used
  • Due to racetrack/plasma ring formation
  • Results in inhomogeneous material loss

Effects:

  • Target outside of racetrack can become insulating (e.g., by oxidation)
  • Can charge up, leading to arcing
  • Racetrack deepens until it (spontaneously) breaks
Target maintenance is very important!

(ii) Effect of Periodic Table

Sputtering rate is element-specific → each target will require element-specific voltages

(iii) Geometry and Crystallographic Effects

Emission of target atoms is reflected by the lattice symmetry

  • In FCC: preferred [110] ejection
  • In BCC: preferred [111] ejection
Whenever the ions penetrate deeper in between the lattice planes, the sputtering yield decreases (atoms cannot get out). [Note: not always the case, especially for semiconductors]

4.7 Plasma Chemistry and Etching

4.7.1 Reaction Rates

Two main types of reactions:

1. Molecule-molecule reaction:
\[A + B \rightarrow C\] (described by "normal" chemistry K(T))
2. Electron-atom/electron-molecule:
\[e^- + A \rightarrow C\] \[\frac{dn_c}{dt} = K(E,T) \cdot n_e n_A\]
Plasma generated reactions can significantly enhance reactivity in chemical vapor processes, even at lower temperatures!

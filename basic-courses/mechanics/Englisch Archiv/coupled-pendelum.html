Coupled Pendulums

Three identical mathematical pendulums (mass m, length l) are connected by two ideal springs of the same spring constant k and move in Earth's homogeneous gravitational field. The length of each unloaded spring equals the distance between the suspension points of the two pendulums it connects.

1. Formulate the Lagrangian for small displacements.

2. Derive the equations of motion.

3. Show through calculation that the eigenfrequencies of the system are:

\[ \omega_1^2 = \frac{g}{l} + \frac{k}{m}, \quad \omega_2^2 = \frac{g}{l}, \quad \omega_3^2 = \frac{g}{l} + \frac{3k}{m} \tag{17} \]
Show Solution

1. Lagrangian:

Kinetic energy in matrix form:

\[ T = \frac{1}{2}\dot{\vec{\alpha}}\cdot M\dot{\vec{\alpha}} = \frac{1}{2}\dot{\vec{\alpha}}\cdot\begin{pmatrix}ml^2 & 0 & 0\\0 & ml^2 & 0\\0 & 0 & ml^2\end{pmatrix}\dot{\vec{\alpha}} \tag{18} \]

Potential energy including gravitational and spring terms:

\[ U = \frac{1}{2}\vec{\alpha}\cdot\begin{pmatrix}mgl+kl^2 & -kl^2 & 0\\-kl^2 & mgl+2kl^2 & -kl^2\\0 & -kl^2 & mgl+kl^2\end{pmatrix}\vec{\alpha} \tag{19} \]

2. Equations of Motion:

\[ M\ddot{\vec{\alpha}} + A\vec{\alpha} = 0 \tag{20} \]

3. Eigenfrequencies:

From characteristic equation:

\[ \det(A - \omega^2M) = 0 \tag{21} \]

Leads to the stated eigenfrequencies after calculation.

Centrifugal Governor

A point m₂ moves along a vertical axis and is connected by a massless rod of length R to two masses m₁. The system is suspended at point A by two additional massless rods of length R and rotates with constant angular velocity ω around the axis. The mass points are subject to gravity.

1. Choose ϑ as the generalized coordinate. Determine the Lagrangian of the system.

2. Let m₂ = 0. Show that ϑ satisfies the equation of motion:

\[ \ddot{\vartheta} = \left(\omega^2\cos(\vartheta) - \frac{g}{R}\right)\sin(\vartheta) \tag{1} \]

3. What condition must the angular velocity ω satisfy for the centrifugal governor to rise up, i.e., for ϑ = 0 to be unstable to small perturbations? Develop the equation of motion around ϑ = 0 up to second order.

Show Solution

Part 1:

For z₁ = R cos(ϑ), z₂ = 2R cos(ϑ) ⇒ \(\dot{z}_2 = -2R\dot{\vartheta}\sin(\vartheta)\)

\begin{align} T &= 2\cdot\frac{m_1}{2}(R^2\sin^2(\vartheta)\omega^2 + R^2\dot{\vartheta}^2) + \frac{m_2}{2}\dot{z}_2^2 \tag{2} \\ &= m_1(R^2\sin^2(\vartheta)\omega^2 + R^2\dot{\vartheta}^2) + 2m_2R^2\dot{\vartheta}^2\sin^2(\vartheta) \tag{3} \end{align}

Setting the zero point of gravitational potential at height of point A:

\[ U = -2m_1gz_1 - m_2gz_2 = -2m_1gR\cos(\vartheta) - 2m_2gR\cos(\vartheta) \tag{4} \]

Part 2:

For m₂ = 0, the equation of motion becomes:

\[ 2m_1R^2\ddot{\vartheta} - 2m_1R^2\omega^2\sin(\vartheta)\cos(\vartheta) + 2m_1gR\sin(\vartheta) = 0 \tag{5} \]

Part 3:

Developing around ϑ = 0:

\begin{align} \ddot{\vartheta} &\approx (\omega^2 - \frac{g}{R})\vartheta \tag{6} \\ \ddot{\vartheta} + (\frac{g}{R} - \omega^2)\vartheta &= 0 \tag{7} \end{align}

For \(\frac{g}{R} - \omega^2 > 0\), this corresponds to harmonic oscillator, force counteracts displacement from ϑ = 0 ⇒ stable against displacements!

For \(\frac{g}{R} - \omega^2 < 0\), force supports displacement ⇒ unstable against displacements, centrifugal governor rises up for \(\omega^2 > \frac{g}{R}\)!

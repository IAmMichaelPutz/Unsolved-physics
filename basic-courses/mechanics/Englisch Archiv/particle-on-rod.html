Particle on a Rod

A particle of mass m is constrained by a force to move frictionlessly along a massless rod. The rod rotates at a fixed angle θ to the z-axis with constant angular velocity ω. No other forces act!

1. Derive the Lagrangian of the particle explicitly in spherical coordinates.

2. Determine the equation of motion for the radius r(t) from the Lagrangian and solve it with initial conditions r(0) = r₀ and ṙ(0) = 0. Sketch the motion of the particle.

Show Solution

1. Kinetic Energy in Spherical Coordinates:

\begin{align} T &= \frac{m}{2}(\dot{r}^2 + r^2\sin^2(\theta)\dot{\phi}^2 + r^2\dot{\theta}^2) \tag{1} \\ &= \frac{m}{2}(\dot{r}^2 + r^2\sin^2(\theta)\omega^2) \tag{2} \end{align}

Potential energy (no forces acting!):

\[ U = 0 \tag{3} \]

Therefore, the Lagrangian:

\[ \mathcal{L} = T - U = \frac{m}{2}(\dot{r}^2 + r^2\sin^2(\theta)\omega^2) \tag{4} \]

2. Equation of Motion:

\begin{align} \frac{d}{dt}\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\dot{r}} - \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial r} &= m\ddot{r} - mr\sin^2(\theta)\omega^2 = 0 \tag{5} \\ \ddot{r} - \omega^2\sin^2(\theta)r &= 0 \tag{6} \end{align}

Solution with given initial conditions:

\[ r(t) = r_0\cosh(\omega\sin(\theta)t) \tag{7} \]

The particle moves on a spiral path at fixed azimuthal angle θ with exponentially growing radius r(t).

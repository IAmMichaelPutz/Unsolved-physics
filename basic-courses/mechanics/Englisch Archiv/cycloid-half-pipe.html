Cycloid Half-Pipe (***)

A mass point slides frictionlessly in Earth's gravitational field on an inverted cycloid (similar to a half-pipe). This cycloid can be parameterized by:

\[ \mathbf{r}(\phi) = \begin{pmatrix} x(\phi) \\ y(\phi) \end{pmatrix} = a\begin{pmatrix} \phi - \sin\phi \\ 1 + \cos\phi \end{pmatrix}, \quad 0 \leq \phi \leq 2\pi \]

a) Determine the Lagrangian using φ as generalized coordinate.

b) Show that this leads to the equation of motion:

\[ \ddot{\phi} + \frac{1}{2}\dot{\phi}^2\cot\frac{\phi}{2} - \frac{g}{2a}\cot\frac{\phi}{2} = 0 \]

and use the substitution u = cos(φ/2) to drastically simplify this equation.

Hint: \(\cot\frac{\phi}{2} = \frac{\sin\phi}{1-\cos\phi}\)

c) Give the general solution of the equation of motion and the solution for φ(t=0) = φ₀ > 0 and φ̇(0) = 0. What are the equations for x(t) and y(t)? What distinguishes this system?

Show Solution

a) Lagrangian:

\begin{align} U &= mgy = mga(1 + \cos\phi) \tag{1} \\ T &= \frac{1}{2}m(\dot{x}^2 + \dot{y}^2) = \frac{1}{2}ma^2[\dot{\phi}^2(1-\cos\phi)^2 + \dot{\phi}^2\sin^2\phi] \tag{2} \\ &= ma^2\dot{\phi}^2(1-\cos\phi) \tag{3} \\ L &= T - U = ma^2\dot{\phi}^2(1-\cos\phi) - mga(1+\cos\phi) \tag{4} \end{align}

b) Equation of Motion:

From Euler-Lagrange equation:

\begin{align} \frac{d}{dt}\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot{\phi}} &= 2ma^2[\ddot{\phi}(1-\cos\phi) + \dot{\phi}^2\sin\phi] \tag{5} \\ \frac{\partial L}{\partial\phi} &= ma^2\dot{\phi}^2\sin\phi + mga\sin\phi \tag{6} \end{align}

With substitution u = cos(φ/2):

\[ \ddot{u} + \frac{g}{4a}u = 0 \tag{7} \]

c) Solution:

General solution:

\[ u(t) = A\cos(\omega t + \psi_0), \quad \omega = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\frac{g}{a}} \tag{8} \]

With initial conditions:

\[ \phi(t) = 2\arccos\left(\cos\frac{\phi_0}{2}\cos\omega t\right) \tag{9} \]

Special property: Time to reach bottom point is independent of starting position:

\[ t = \frac{\pi}{2\omega} \tag{10} \]

This property is called tautochrone - all particles reach the bottom in the same time regardless of starting height.

Connected Mass Points

Two mass points m and M are connected by a massless string of constant length l = r + s. The mass can rotate on the plane with a varying partial length r. The string leads through a hole in the plane to M, where mass m hangs on the taut string (with the also varying partial length s = l - r).

1. Set up the Lagrangian of the system in suitable coordinates.

2. Determine the equations of motion and constants of motion. State under which conditions M accelerates upward or downward.

Show Solution

We choose as generalized coordinates the length r and angle φ.

\begin{align} T &= \frac{M}{2}\dot{s}^2 + \frac{m}{2}(\dot{r}^2 + r^2\dot{\phi}^2) \tag{1} \\ &= \frac{M}{2}\left(\frac{d}{dt}(l-r)\right)^2 + \frac{m}{2}(\dot{r}^2 + r^2\dot{\phi}^2) \tag{2} \\ &= \frac{m+M}{2}\dot{r}^2 + \frac{m}{2}r^2\dot{\phi}^2 \tag{3} \end{align}

Setting the zero point of gravitational potential in the plane:

\[ U = -Mg(r-l) \tag{4} \]

The Lagrangian of the system:

\[ \mathcal{L} = \frac{M+m}{2}\dot{r}^2 + \frac{m}{2}r^2\dot{\phi}^2 - Mg(r-l) \tag{5} \]

Constants of Motion:

φ is cyclic coordinate ⇔ \(\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\phi} = 0\) ⇔ \(\frac{d}{dt}\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\dot{\phi}} = 0\) ⇔ \(\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\dot{\phi}} = mr^2\dot{\phi}\) is constant (angular momentum of m in the plane).

Equation of Motion for r:

\[ (M+m)\ddot{r} - mr\dot{\phi}^2 + Mg = 0 \tag{6} \]

M accelerates upward ⇔ \(\ddot{r} > 0\) ⇔ \(mr\dot{\phi}^2 > Mg\) (the centrifugal force of m must be greater than the gravitational force of M)

Lagrangian Mechanics, Systems of Oscillations

1. Planar Double Pendulum (*)

Consider a planar double pendulum in three-dimensional space.

a) Show that there are four holonomic constraints for this system. Formulate these constraints. How many independent degrees of freedom remain? What are the appropriate generalized coordinates q_i?

b) Express the kinetic energy

\[ T = \frac{1}{2}m_1\dot{\mathbf{r}}_1^2 + \frac{1}{2}m_2\dot{\mathbf{r}}_2^2 \]

in terms of the generalized coordinates. Show that:

\[ T = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i,j}a_{ij}(q)\dot{q}_i\dot{q}_j \]

and determine the matrix a_ij(q).

Hint: sin α sin β + cos α cos β = cos(α - β)

c) Give the potential energy in terms of generalized coordinates q_i.

Show Solution

a) Constraints and Degrees of Freedom:

The system initially has 3 + 3 = 6 degrees of freedom. Constraints:

\begin{align} z_1 &= 0 \tag{1} \\ z_2 &= 0 \tag{2} \\ (x_1 - x_0)^2 + (y_1 - y_0)^2 - l_1^2 &= 0 \tag{3} \\ (x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2 - l_2^2 &= 0 \tag{4} \end{align}

Therefore 6 - 4 = 2 independent degrees of freedom remain. The appropriate generalized coordinates are the angles θ₁ and θ₂:

\begin{align} x_1 &= x_0 - l_1\cos\theta_1 \tag{5} \\ y_1 &= y_0 + l_1\sin\theta_1 \tag{6} \\ x_2 &= x_1 - l_2\cos\theta_2 = x_0 - l_1\cos\theta_1 - l_2\cos\theta_2 \tag{7} \\ y_2 &= y_1 + l_2\sin\theta_2 = y_0 + l_1\sin\theta_1 + l_2\sin\theta_2 \tag{8} \end{align}

b) Kinetic Energy:

\begin{align} T_1 &= \frac{1}{2}m_1l_1^2\dot{\theta}_1^2 \tag{9} \\ T_2 &= \frac{1}{2}m_2[l_1^2\dot{\theta}_1^2 + l_2^2\dot{\theta}_2^2 + 2l_1l_2\dot{\theta}_1\dot{\theta}_2\cos(\theta_1-\theta_2)] \tag{10} \end{align}

Total kinetic energy matrix form:

\[ T = \frac{1}{2}(\dot{\theta}_1, \dot{\theta}_2)\begin{pmatrix}(m_1+m_2)l_1^2 & m_2l_1l_2\cos(\theta_1-\theta_2) \\ m_2l_1l_2\cos(\theta_1-\theta_2) & m_2l_2^2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}\dot{\theta}_1 \\ \dot{\theta}_2\end{pmatrix} \tag{11} \]

c) Potential Energy:

\begin{align} U &= m_1gx_1 + m_2gx_2 \tag{12} \\ &= (m_1 + m_2)gx_0 - (m_1 + m_2)gl_1\cos\theta_1 - m_2gl_2\cos\theta_2 \tag{13} \end{align}

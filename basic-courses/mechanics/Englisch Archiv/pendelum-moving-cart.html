Pendulum on a Moving Cart

A planar pendulum of mass m hangs on a rigid, massless rod of length l in Earth's homogeneous gravitational field. The pendulum is suspended from a mass M that can move frictionlessly along a horizontal axis. The pendulum can only swing in the plane spanned by this axis and the direction of Earth's gravitational field. No other forces act.

1. Set up the Lagrangian of the system in suitable coordinates.

2. State the conserved quantities of the system including their physical meaning.

Show Solution

1. Lagrangian:

We choose as generalized coordinates the x-position x = x_M of mass M and angle φ.

\begin{align} x_m &= x + l\sin(\phi) \tag{8} \\ \dot{x}_m &= \dot{x} + l\dot{\phi}\cos(\phi) \tag{9} \\ y_m &= l\cos(\phi) \tag{10} \\ \dot{y}_m &= -l\dot{\phi}\sin(\phi) \tag{11} \end{align}

Kinetic Energy:

\begin{align} T &= \frac{M}{2}\dot{x}^2 + \frac{m}{2}(\dot{x}_m^2 + \dot{y}_m^2) \tag{12} \\ &= \frac{M+m}{2}\dot{x}^2 + ml\dot{x}\dot{\phi}\cos(\phi) + \frac{m}{2}l^2\dot{\phi}^2 \tag{13} \end{align}

Setting zero point of gravitational potential on x-axis:

\[ U = -mgy_m = -mgl\cos(\phi) \tag{14} \]

Therefore the Lagrangian:

\[ L = \frac{M+m}{2}\dot{x}^2 + ml\dot{x}\dot{\phi}\cos(\phi) + \frac{m}{2}l^2\dot{\phi}^2 + mgl\cos(\phi) \tag{15} \]

2. Conserved Quantities:

Since only conservative gravitational force acts, energy E = T + U is conserved

Additionally, L doesn't depend explicitly on x, so x is cyclic coordinate and conjugate momentum is conserved:

\[ \frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot{x}} = (M+m)\dot{x} + ml\dot{\phi}\cos(\phi) = M\dot{x} + m\dot{x}_m \tag{16} \]

⇒ Total momentum/center of mass momentum in x-direction is conserved

Blocks in a Shaft (***)

Two blocks of equal mass m are connected by a rigid, massless rod of length l and move frictionlessly along the path shown in the figure under the influence of gravity.

a) What are the constraints? Set up the Lagrangian for the generalized coordinate α(t).

b) Show that the Euler-Lagrange equation can be written as:

\[ \ddot{\alpha} + \frac{g}{l}\cos\alpha = 0 \]

c) Determine the velocity of the mass falling along the y-axis as a function of angle α. The initial condition follows from a small displacement from unstable equilibrium at α = 90°. Calculate ẏ at α = 45°, α = 0°, and α = -45° for l = 1 m, g ≈ 10 m/s².

Show Solution

a) Constraints and Lagrangian:

Constraints: x₁ = y₂ = z₁ = z₂ = 0 and y₁² + x₂² - l² = 0

\begin{align} y_1 &= l\sin\alpha \tag{1} \\ x_2 &= l\cos\alpha \tag{2} \\ T &= \frac{1}{2}m(\dot{y}_1^2 + \dot{x}_2^2) = \frac{1}{2}ml^2\dot{\alpha}^2 \tag{3} \\ U &= mgy_1 = mgl\sin\alpha \tag{4} \\ L &= T - U = \frac{1}{2}ml^2\dot{\alpha}^2 - mgl\sin\alpha \tag{5} \end{align}

b) Equation of Motion:

\[ ml^2\ddot{\alpha} + mgl\cos\alpha = 0 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \ddot{\alpha} + \frac{g}{l}\cos\alpha = 0 \tag{6} \]

c) Velocity Analysis:

Using energy conservation:

\begin{align} \dot{\alpha}^2 + \frac{2g}{l}\sin\alpha &= \frac{2g}{l} \tag{7} \\ \dot{\alpha} &= -\sqrt{\frac{2g}{l}(1-\sin\alpha)} \tag{8} \\ \dot{y}_1(\alpha) &= l\dot{\alpha}\cos\alpha = -\cos\alpha\sqrt{2gl(1-\sin\alpha)} \tag{9} \end{align}

For l = 1 m, g = 10 m/s²:

\begin{align} \dot{y}_1(45°) &= -1.7\text{ m/s} \tag{10} \\ \dot{y}_1(0°) &= -4.5\text{ m/s} \tag{11} \\ \dot{y}_1(-45°) &= -4.1\text{ m/s} \tag{12} \end{align}

7. Spring-Connected Masses on Ring (**)

Three mass points can move frictionlessly on a circular ring with radius R and are connected by three identical springs with spring constant k. Two of the mass points have the same mass m, while the third has mass M = αm, α > 0. No other forces act.

a) Set up the Lagrangian for this system. Use angles φᵢ, i = 1,2,3 as generalized coordinates, defined as displacements from a position determined by equal spring tensions.

b) Set up the equations of motion.

c) Determine the eigenfrequencies.

d) Calculate the corresponding normal modes and interpret them.

e) What symmetry does this system exhibit? What conserved quantity exists due to this symmetry?

Show Solution

a) Lagrangian:

\begin{align} T &= \frac{1}{2}mR^2(\dot{\phi}_1^2 + \dot{\phi}_2^2 + \alpha\dot{\phi}_3^2) \tag{1} \\ U &= \frac{1}{2}kR^2[(\phi_1 - \phi_2)^2 + (\phi_2 - \phi_3)^2 + (\phi_3 - \phi_1)^2] \tag{2} \\ L &= T - U \tag{3} \end{align}

b) Equations of Motion:

\begin{equation} \begin{pmatrix} m & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & m & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & \alpha m \end{pmatrix} \ddot{\vec{\phi}} + k\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & -1 \\ -1 & 2 & -1 \\ -1 & -1 & 2 \end{pmatrix} \vec{\phi} = 0 \tag{4} \end{equation}

c) Eigenfrequencies:

\begin{align} \omega_1^2 &= 0 \tag{5} \\ \omega_2^2 &= 3\frac{k}{m} \tag{6} \\ \omega_3^2 &= \frac{k}{m}\frac{2+\alpha}{\alpha} \tag{7} \end{align}

d) Normal Modes:

\begin{align} \vec{C}_1 &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} \tag{8} \\ \vec{C}_2 &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} \tag{9} \\ \vec{C}_3 &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\sqrt{1+(\frac{2}{\alpha})^2}}\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ -\frac{2}{\alpha} \end{pmatrix} \tag{10} \end{align}

e) Symmetry and Conservation:

- Rotational symmetry: system energy unchanged when all masses are rotated by same angle

- Conserved quantity: total angular momentum l = mR²(2+α)φ̇

- Associated normal mode: C₁ with ω₁ = 0 (uniform rotation)

8. Coupled String Pendulums (***)

Three identical mathematical pendulums (mass m, length l) are connected by two ideal springs of the same spring constant k and move in Earth's homogeneous gravitational field. The length of each unloaded spring equals the distance between the suspension points of the two pendulums it connects.

a) Formulate the Lagrangian for small displacements.

b) Derive the equations of motion.

c) Show that the eigenfrequencies are:

\[ \omega_1^2 = \frac{g}{l} + \frac{k}{m}, \quad \omega_2^2 = \frac{g}{l}, \quad \omega_3^2 = \frac{g}{l} + \frac{3k}{m} \]

d) Calculate the eigenvectors for the two slowest normal modes.

e) Give the fastest normal mode without calculation, with brief justification.

Show Solution

a) Lagrangian:

\begin{align} T &= \frac{1}{2}ml^2(\dot{\alpha}_1^2 + \dot{\alpha}_2^2 + \dot{\alpha}_3^2) \tag{1} \\ U_g &= \frac{mgl}{2}(\alpha_1^2 + \alpha_2^2 + \alpha_3^2) \tag{2} \\ U_k &= \frac{kl^2}{2}(\alpha_1^2 - 2\alpha_1\alpha_2 + 2\alpha_2^2 - 2\alpha_2\alpha_3 + \alpha_3^2) \tag{3} \end{align}

b) Equations of Motion:

\begin{equation} \begin{pmatrix} ml^2 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & ml^2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & ml^2 \end{pmatrix} \ddot{\vec{\alpha}} + \begin{pmatrix} mgl+kl^2 & -kl^2 & 0 \\ -kl^2 & mgl+2kl^2 & -kl^2 \\ 0 & -kl^2 & mgl+kl^2 \end{pmatrix} \vec{\alpha} = 0 \tag{4} \end{equation}

d) Slowest Normal Modes:

\begin{align} \vec{C}_1 &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix} \tag{5} \\ \vec{C}_2 &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} \tag{6} \end{align}

e) Fastest Normal Mode:

\[ \vec{C}_3 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} \tag{7} \]

Justification: Term 3k/m implies all springs must be loaded; middle pendulum must swing opposite to outer ones with double amplitude due to double spring force.

9. Noether's Theorem (*)

Which components of momentum p and angular momentum L are conserved when a mass point moves in a three-dimensional potential whose equipotential surfaces are given by the following cases:

a) Infinite planes parallel to xz-plane

b) Concentric cylindrical shells with cylinder axes on y-axis

c) Concentric spherical surfaces

d) Concentric right circular cones with identical opening angles and x-axis as symmetry axis

e) Concentric ellipsoid surfaces with a = b ≠ c

Show Solution

a) Planes parallel to xz-plane:

  • Translation invariant in x,z: p_x, p_z conserved
  • Rotation invariant around y-axis: L_y conserved

b) Cylindrical shells around y-axis:

  • Translation invariant in y: p_y conserved
  • Rotation invariant around y-axis: L_y conserved

c) Spherical surfaces:

  • Rotation invariant around all axes: Total angular momentum L conserved

d) Cones around x-axis:

  • Rotation invariant around x-axis: L_x conserved

e) Ellipsoids with a = b ≠ c:

  • Rotation invariant around z-axis: L_z conserved

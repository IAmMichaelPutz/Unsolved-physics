3 Mass on Inclined Plane

A mass m is connected to a wedge of mass M by a spring (spring constant k). The wedge has an inclination angle of α and can move frictionless along the horizontal plane.

Set up the Lagrange function of the system in terms of the x-coordinates of the wedge and the spring length s, and determine the equations of motion. Find a cyclic coordinate and its associated conserved quantity.

Show Solution

Let the height of the wedge be h. Using x and s as generalized coordinates, the mass m has the Cartesian coordinates:

\[ (x + s\cos\alpha; h - s\sin\alpha) \tag{1} \]

From this, we can determine the velocity and thus the kinetic energy:

\[ T = \frac{M}{2}\dot{x}^2 + \frac{m}{2}[(\dot{x} + \dot{s}\cos\alpha)^2 + (\dot{s}\sin\alpha)^2] \tag{2} \]

The potential energy consists of the gravitational potential energy of m and the spring potential energy:

\[ V = \frac{k}{2}(s-d)^2 + mg(h - s\sin\alpha) \tag{3} \]

Therefore, the Lagrange function is:

\[ \mathcal{L} = T - V = \frac{M}{2}\dot{x}^2 + \frac{m}{2}[(\dot{x} + \dot{s}\cos\alpha)^2 + (\dot{s}\sin\alpha)^2] - \frac{k}{2}(s-d)^2 - mg(h - s\sin\alpha) \tag{4} \]

The Euler-Lagrange equations:

\begin{align} \frac{d}{dt}\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\dot{x}} - \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial x} &= (M + m)\ddot{x} + m\ddot{s}\cos\alpha = 0 \tag{5} \\ \frac{d}{dt}\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\dot{s}} - \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial s} &= m\ddot{x}\cos\alpha + m\ddot{s} + ks - (kd + mg\sin\alpha) = 0 \tag{6} \end{align}

The Lagrange function does not depend on x, therefore x is cyclic. Thus, the conjugate momentum p_x is a conserved quantity. This is NOT the kinematic momentum p = (M + m)ẋ, but rather:

\[ p_x = \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\dot{x}} = (M + m)\dot{x} + m\dot{s}\cos\alpha = const. \tag{7} \]

as can be seen from the first equation of motion.
